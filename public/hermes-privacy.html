Privacy Policy — Personal Hermes Integration

Last updated: October 1, 2026.

This policy describes the personal Google integration operated by Shahar Faradyan. It does not describe all Hermes installations or the practices of Nous Research. Contact: shaharfar16@gmail.com.

Information accessed

Following explicit Google authorization, the integration can access email content, headers and labels; calendar events and attendee details; contact information; Drive files and metadata; and document and spreadsheet contents. Actual access depends on the permissions granted and the tasks performed. OAuth access and refresh credentials are retained to support authorized API access without signing in for each task.

Use of information

Information is used to carry out the account owner's requested assistant tasks, such as summarizing messages, sending email, creating or updating events, and reading or editing documents. Relevant information may appear in assistant responses, task artifacts and conversation history.

Processing and disclosure

Relevant task information may be transmitted to the configured third-party AI provider for inference. Responses sent through a messaging channel are also processed by that channel's provider. Requested emails, invitations and shared files disclose information to their intended recipients. Google processes API requests, and the operator's hosting infrastructure processes stored data. Data is not promised to remain exclusively on the operator's server.

The operator does not sell Google user data or use it for advertising. Google-derived data must not be used to train generalized AI models. Provider settings and contractual terms must support this restriction before Google-derived content is processed by an AI service.

Retention and security

Credentials and task records are stored in the operator's environment. Access is restricted using available account and filesystem controls. This policy does not claim that every stored record is encrypted. Conversation history, generated files, logs and backups may retain task information; this integration has no promised automatic deletion interval. External providers' retention is governed by their applicable terms and configuration.

Revocation and deletion

You can revoke authorization through Google Account connections. This prevents further use of the revoked grant but does not delete existing records or recall messages and invitations already sent. Contact the operator to request deletion of credentials and retained task data under the operator's control. Copies already delivered to recipients or retained by external services may require separate deletion requests.

Google API policy

The integration's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs must adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements. This page is not evidence of Google verification or a security certification.

Changes

Changes to the integration's data practices should be reflected in this policy and reviewed before granting or expanding access.
