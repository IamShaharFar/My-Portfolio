Hermes — Personal Google Integration

This integration is operated by Shahar Faradyan for personal use. It connects Shahar's instance of the Hermes AI assistant to Google Workspace. It is not a public sign-up service or the official website of Hermes or Nous Research.

Purpose and access

At the account owner's direction, the assistant can read and organize email, send messages, manage calendar events and invitations, look up contacts, and work with files, documents and spreadsheets.

The current Google authorization requests Gmail read, send and modify access; Calendar access; Drive access; read-only Contacts access; and Docs and Sheets access. These permissions are broader than calendar-only access. They do not authorize the assistant to administer a Google Cloud project.

AI processing

Information needed for an assistant task can be processed on the operator's server and transmitted to the configured AI provider. This integration is not a local-only data processing system. Read the privacy policy before granting access.

Control

Only an account owner who explicitly authorizes the integration can connect their Google account. Access can be withdrawn in Google Account connections. Revoking access does not automatically erase previously retained assistant records.
